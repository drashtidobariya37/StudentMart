Campus Trust & Safety Protocol

Student Safety Centre

Essential protocols and policies to keep campus peer-to-peer commerce safe, transparent, and pleasant for every student.

1. The Daylight Campus Handoff Protocol

Because Student Mart is designed for college communities, you should never meet a stranger in secluded off-campus alleys or private rooms. Always schedule exchanges during daylight hours in high-traffic campus locations:

Central Library Steps Public, monitored, safe daylight area
Main Student Canteen Busy social hub during daytime
Student Activity Center Well-lit campus recreation area

2. Prohibited Items Policy

Any user attempting to list items from this list will face immediate account suspension and notification to campus administrative authorities:

  • Unauthorized Exam & Cheating Material: Solved live test papers, answer leaks, proxy attendance devices, or spy cameras.
  • Institutional / Hostel Property: Library books with college barcodes, lab equipment belonging to departmental labs, or hostel furniture.
  • Hazardous Chemicals or Unregulated Lab Reagents: Flammable chemicals, lab acids, or restricted biological specimens.
  • Weapons & Dangerous Objects: Knives, air guns, pepper spray, or illegal fireworks.
  • Substances, Alcohol & Vapes: Any regulated intoxicants, cigarettes, e-cigarettes, or tobacco products.

3. Anti-Fraud & UPI Warning

Remember: You NEVER enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Scammers frequently send a Google Pay or PhonePe payment request claiming they are paying an advance. Entering your UPI PIN deducts money from your bank account!
  • Do not send advance deposits or token payments before verifying the physical item in person.
  • Test electronic devices (calculators, laptops, chargers) before marking an order as Completed.
  • Inspect textbooks to ensure essential chapters and appendices are not torn or missing.

4. How to Report Suspicious Activity

If you see a suspicious listing, inappropriate message, or prohibited item:

  1. Click the Report Listing button on any product detail page.
  2. Or use the Report User button inside any active message conversation.
  3. Choose the appropriate violation category and provide specific context.
  4. Student Mart administrators review reports within 12 hours and can remove listings or suspend accounts.
