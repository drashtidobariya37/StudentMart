Student Privacy Policy

Data minimization, privacy-by-design, and student identity protection.

1. Minimal Personal Data Collection

We only collect data necessary to facilitate safe campus commerce: display name, institutional or contact email, affiliated college, course/year, and optional student ID. We do not track location coordinates or sell personal student metrics to data brokers.

2. Privacy by Default (No Public Phone Numbers)

Unlike classified ad websites where student phone numbers are scraped by spammers, Student Mart hides phone numbers and personal email addresses from public product pages by default. All initial buyer-seller inquiries happen through authenticated in-app message threads.

3. Password Security & Sessions

All user passwords are stored using salted cryptographic password hashes generated by Werkzeug. Plaintext passwords are never logged, stored, or visible to system administrators. Sessions use HTTP-only, SameSite-protected cookies.

4. Data Deletion

Students may delete their active listings at any time or contact support to remove their account history upon graduation.
