Terms of Service & Code of Conduct

Last updated: October 2026 • Student Marketplace Operating Guidelines

1. Eligibility & Community Scope

Student Mart is intended for enrolled students, researchers, faculty, and campus staff of participating Indian higher educational institutions. Users must provide accurate details regarding their campus affiliation and student roll number where requested.

2. Listing Obligations

Sellers must represent item conditions accurately (Brand New, Like New, Good, Fair) and disclose defects or missing pages in books. Listing stolen property, institutional property, or items from the Prohibited Items Policy is strictly forbidden and results in permanent ban.

3. Direct Payments & Escrow Clarification

Student Mart offers simulated escrow workflows for demonstration and learning purposes. In real-world campus interactions, direct UPI transfers (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm) or cash handoffs are direct bilateral transfers between buyer and seller. Student Mart does not act as a custodial bank or legal escrow agency unless explicitly regulated and authorized.

4. Disputes & Administrative Arbitration

Either party in an order may submit a formal dispute prior to marking an item as Completed. Campus student administrators will review message histories and submitted photos to reach a fair resolution.

5. Safety Disclaimer

Users agree to follow campus security guidelines and conduct meetings in daylight at designated public college spots.
