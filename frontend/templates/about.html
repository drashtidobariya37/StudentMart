About Student Mart

An authentic multi-college peer-to-peer marketplace built to solve campus textbook waste and expensive student essentials in India.

1. Project Background & Problem Statement

Every academic year across Indian engineering and university campuses, senior students finish their semesters with expensive textbooks (like Higher Engineering Mathematics by B.S. Grewal, Operating Systems by Silberschatz), mini drafters, engineering drawing sheets, Casio fx-991EX calculators, and hostel appliances that they no longer need. Meanwhile, incoming juniors purchase the exact same items at full retail price.

Traditional general classified platforms like OLX or Quikr are filled with commercial resellers, delivery courier hassles, and scam risks. Student Mart was developed to create a dedicated, hyper-local, college-verified ecosystem where students hand off goods directly inside campus premises.

2. Architecture & Technology Stack

Backend Layer

  • Python 3.11 with Flask Web Framework
  • Flask-SQLAlchemy (ORM) with SQLite database
  • Werkzeug secure password hashing
  • HMAC-SHA256 signature verification
  • Custom CSRF defense & Rate-Limiting

Frontend Layer

  • Semantic HTML5 & Modern Responsive CSS3
  • Custom Navy & Terracotta Theme
  • Pure Vanilla JavaScript (No heavy frameworks)
  • Real-time chat polling with auto-scroll
  • Client-side preview + Server-side validation
Payment & Escrow Model: Integrated with official Razorpay Sandbox APIs (when test credentials are provided). To guarantee zero dead-ends during evaluation, the platform includes a built-in Campus Safe Demo Escrow engine with realistic state machine transitions: PENDING → ACCEPTED → COMPLETED / DISPUTED.

3. Trust, Safety & Privacy Design Principles

  • Data Minimization: Student phone numbers and personal emails are never broadcast publicly on listing cards. All initial communication occurs through private, encrypted-in-transit buyer-seller chat threads.
  • No Fabricated Trust Badges: The platform does not display fake 5-star ratings or unverified seller badges. "Verified Student" badges are strictly tied to confirmed university email domains or admin audit.
  • Daylight Handoffs: Design guidelines strongly encourage all transactions to occur at officially recognized public meeting spots (e.g. Central Library, Main Canteen, Student Activity Center).
  • Admin Moderation Workflow: A protected administrative portal allows reviewing reported items, moderating questionable content, and arbitrating buyer-seller disputes.
